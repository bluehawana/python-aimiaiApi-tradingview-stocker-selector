## 📈 Expected Results

### Realistic Expectations

- **Not every Golden Cross = 6x gain**
- **Success rate: ~30-40%** of Golden Cross signals lead to significant gains
- **Average gain: 50-200%** for successful picks
- **Time horizon: 3-6 months** from Golden Cross to peak

### Risk Management

1. **Diversification**

   - Don't put all money in one stock
   - Spread across 3-5 opportunities
   - Mix of different sub-sectors

2. **Position Sizing**

   - Start with small position (10-20%)
   - Add on confirmation (Golden Cross + news catalyst)
   - Take profits gradually (not all at once)

3. **Stop Loss**

   - Set stop loss at support price (MCD indicator)
   - Exit if Death Cross appears
   - Cut losses if sector sentiment changes

4. **Profit Taking**
   - Take 30% profit at 2x
   - Take 30% profit at 3x
   - Let 40% run for potential 6x+
